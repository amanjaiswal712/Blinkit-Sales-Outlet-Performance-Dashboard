# Blinkit Sales & Outlet Performance Dashboard 🛒📊

## 📌 Project Overview
This project features an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to conduct a comprehensive analysis of Blinkit's sales performance, customer satisfaction, and outlet effectiveness. The goal of this analysis is to identify key sales insights and opportunities for business optimization using various KPIs and data visualizations based on a Kaggle dataset.

## 🌟 STAR Methodology Breakdown

### **Situation**
In the highly competitive quick-commerce industry, platforms like Blinkit must continuously monitor their sales metrics and understand performance across various outlet types and geographical locations. To make data-driven decisions, stakeholders require a consolidated view of overall revenue alongside granular details regarding item categories, store sizes, and customer ratings.

### **Task**
The objective was to build a dynamic Power BI dashboard that tracks four primary KPIs:
* **Total Sales:** The overall revenue generated from all items sold.
* **Average Sales:** The average revenue per sale.
* **Number of Items:** The total count of different items sold.
* **Average Rating:** The average customer rating for items sold.

Additionally, the dashboard needed to segment these metrics across various business dimensions, including fat content, item type, outlet size, outlet establishment age, and specific outlet locations.

### **Action**
I processed and modeled the sales data in Power BI, creating calculated measures for the primary KPIs. To provide deep, actionable insights, I developed the following visualizations:
* **Donut/Pie Charts:** Analyzed the percentage of sales by Outlet Size to understand the correlation between retail space and revenue generation. 
* **Bar/Column Charts:** Visualized Total Sales by Item Type and Fat Content, assessing how these categories impact secondary KPIs like Average Rating and Number of Items.
* **Trend Analysis:** Evaluated Total Sales by Outlet Establishment to determine how the age or type of an outlet influences its performance.
* **Geographic & Matrix Visuals:** Mapped sales across various Outlet Locations and created a comprehensive matrix detailing all key metrics broken down by Outlet Type.

### **Result**
The final dashboard provides a clear, interactive overview of Blinkit's sales operations. Stakeholders can now seamlessly filter data to compare total sales across different outlets, geographical locations, and item types. This empowers the business to identify high-performing outlet formats, understand regional sales differences, and tailor sales strategies based on customer purchasing patterns.

## 💡 Key Insights Generated
* **Sales Drivers:** Clear visibility into which item types and fat content categories drive the highest volume and revenue.
* **Store Format Strategy:** Quantifiable correlation between outlet size, establishment age, and total sales output.
* **Geographic Performance:** Direct tracking of how sales and average customer ratings fluctuate based on specific outlet locations and the type of outlet fulfilling the order.
